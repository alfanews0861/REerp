# REOS Product Requirement Specification

# Volume 6

## Site Visit, Vehicle, Driver & Field Operations Engine

Version: 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Objective

Site Visit is the most important sales activity in Open Plot marketing.

The objective is to help the sales team convert an Opportunity into a
Booking.

The software must support the business without forcing a fixed process.
