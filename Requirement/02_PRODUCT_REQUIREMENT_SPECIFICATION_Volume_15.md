# REOS Product Requirement Specification

# Volume 15

## MVP Scope, Roadmap, Acceptance & Go-Live

Version: 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. MVP Objective

Deliver a production-ready Real Estate Enterprise Operating System
(REOS) focused on Open Plot Marketing Companies while remaining
configurable for future expansion.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   Site Visits
-   Vehicle Management
-   Expenses
-   Booking
-   Payments
-   Registration

# 9. Final Acceptance

The product is accepted when:

✓ All MVP modules function correctly

✓ Business workflows execute successfully

✓ Reports match business expectations

✓ AI insights operate correctly

✓ Security validation completed

✓ Production deployment successful

✓ User Acceptance Testing approved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Vision

REOS is designed as a configurable enterprise platform that begins with
Telugu States open plot marketing companies and scales to support the
wider Indian real estate industry.
