# REOS Product Requirement Specification

# Volume 10

## AI Engine, Analytics & Decision Support System

Version: 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Objective

The AI Engine must act as a business assistant for management, sales
teams and operations. It should analyze data, identify patterns and
recommend actions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. AI Architecture

Data Sources

-   CRM
-   Leads
-   Opportunities
-   Site Visits
-   Bookings
-   Payments
-   Inventory
-   Vehicles
-   Expenses
-   Customer Support

Outputs

-   Recommendations
-   Predictions
-   Alerts
-   Summaries
-   Reports

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Executive AI Assistant

Daily Brief

-   New Leads
-   Follow-ups Due
-   Site Visits
-   Bookings
-   Collections
-   Pending Registrations

Ask AI

Examples:

-   Which executive performed best this week?
-   Which project needs attention?
-   Which leads are likely to convert?
-   Which vehicles are underutilized?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Lead Intelligence

AI should detect:

-   Hot Leads
-   Warm Leads
-   Cold Leads
-   Duplicate Leads
-   Lost Opportunities
-   Dormant Leads

Recommendations

-   Best follow-up time
-   Best communication channel
-   Executive reassignment suggestion
-   Next best action

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Sales Intelligence

KPIs

-   Conversion %
-   Revenue per Executive
-   Revenue per Agent
-   Revenue per Campaign
-   Revenue per Project

AI identifies:

-   Top performers
-   Underperformers
-   Bottlenecks
-   Missed opportunities

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Inventory Intelligence

AI analyzes

-   Fast moving inventory
-   Slow moving inventory
-   Unsold plots
-   Price sensitivity
-   Block-wise demand
-   Facing-wise demand

Recommendations

-   Increase price
-   Reduce price
-   Launch campaign
-   Bundle inventory

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Financial Intelligence

AI monitors

-   Cash Flow
-   Collections
-   Outstanding
-   Commission Liability
-   Expense Trends

Predictions

-   Monthly revenue
-   Collection forecast
-   Cash requirement

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Operations Intelligence

Vehicle

-   Idle vehicles
-   Cost per trip
-   Fuel anomalies

Executives

-   Missed follow-ups
-   Visit productivity
-   Booking ratio

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Customer Intelligence

AI detects

-   High value customers
-   Referral opportunities
-   Repeat investors
-   Complaint trends

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Alerts

Examples

-   High priority lead ignored
-   Price updated
-   Large payment received
-   Registration overdue
-   Vehicle insurance expiring
-   Driver license expiring

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Decision Support

Instead of only showing reports, AI should recommend:

-   Which project to promote
-   Which campaign to stop
-   Which executive needs support
-   Which inventory to prioritize
-   Expected monthly performance

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Acceptance Criteria

✓ Daily AI Brief

✓ Predictive Analytics

✓ Business Recommendations

✓ Natural Language Queries

✓ Executive Decision Support

End of Volume 10
