# REOS Product Requirement Specification

# Volume 8

## Customer Experience, Communication & Notification Engine

Version: 1.0

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Objective

Provide a complete post-booking communication platform for customers,
management and sales teams.

The platform must improve customer confidence while reducing manual
follow-up.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Customer Support

Support Request Types

-   General Enquiry
-   Payment Query
-   Registration Query
-   Document Request
-   Development Update
-   Complaint
-   Suggestion
-   Other

Each request stores:

-   Ticket Number
-   Customer
-   Category
-   Priority
-   Assigned To
-   Status
-   Resolution
-   Timeline

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Ticket Status

-   New
-   Assigned
-   In Progress
-   Waiting for Customer
-   Resolved
-   Closed

Every update is recorded in the audit timeline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Customer Document Center

Management uploads documents.

Supported Categories

-   Layout Documents
-   Payment Receipts
-   Registration Documents
-   Brochures
-   Price Lists
-   Development Photos
-   Other Files

Customers can only view documents permitted to them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Communication Hub

Supported Channels

-   SMS
-   WhatsApp
-   Email
-   Push Notification
-   In-App Notification

Communication Templates

-   Lead Follow-up
-   Visit Reminder
-   Booking Confirmation
-   Payment Reminder
-   Registration Reminder
-   Birthday Wishes
-   Festival Greetings
-   Promotional Campaigns

Templates are configurable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Notification Engine

Triggers

-   New Lead
-   Follow-up Due
-   Visit Scheduled
-   Booking Created
-   Payment Received
-   Payment Due
-   Registration Completed
-   New Announcement

Notifications support:

-   Individual Users
-   Teams
-   Branches
-   Entire Company

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Calendar & Reminder Engine

Supports

-   Personal Calendar
-   Team Calendar
-   Visit Calendar
-   Follow-up Calendar
-   Registration Calendar

Reminder Types

-   One Time
-   Daily
-   Weekly
-   Monthly
-   Custom

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Internal Announcement Center

Management may publish:

-   Circulars
-   Offers
-   Price Updates
-   Project Updates
-   Holiday Notices
-   Training Programs

Supports attachments and acknowledgement tracking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Customer Experience Dashboard

Displays

-   Open Tickets
-   Average Resolution Time
-   Customer Response Time
-   Pending Document Requests
-   Satisfaction Rating

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. AI Customer Assistant

AI assists with

-   Frequently Asked Questions
-   Payment Explanation
-   Booking Status
-   Document Guidance
-   Next Step Recommendation

AI escalates complex requests to human staff.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Acceptance Criteria

✓ Ticket management

✓ Multi-channel communication

✓ Template management

✓ Notification automation

✓ Calendar reminders

✓ Customer document center

✓ AI assisted customer support

End of Volume 8
